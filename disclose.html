How listings work

Manifest First is a free checklist for Houston-area kitchens. The customer never pays this directory. No shop pays for a listing on this page.

Phones are copied from the company’s own public site. A listing is not a recommendation, a license check, or a quote. Confirm the number, the service area, and the paperwork before you book.

Governing law for this directory is Texas, the first-metro state. This page is not legal advice and not a city inspection.

To correct a number, reply to the listing note or use the contact published on your own site.
