Is a paid invoice enough for the inspector?

Treat it as no until you have the waste manifest. Operators in Houston describe the invoice as proof of payment and the manifest as the chain from the trap to disposal. City of Houston Solid Waste says commercial grease traps must be cleaned every 90 days unless Houston Health Department approves a waiver.

What do owners actually ask?

Whether the truck fully pumped the trap, whether the paper is a manifest or only a bill, and whether a cracked interceptor is a pump visit or a replacement. Those are the questions on this checklist.

Where is the city statement?

City of Houston Solid Waste, cooking-oil page: commercial kitchens must install grease traps that must be cleaned every 90 days unless a waiver is submitted and approved by the Houston Health Department. houstontx.gov cooking-oil page.
