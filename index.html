Manifest First

Before you pay a Houston grease-trap bill, ask whether the truck fully pumped the interceptor and whether you get a waste manifest — not just an invoice.

Kitchen owners keep getting a paid invoice and still failing the next visit because the file has no manifest, no gallons, and no disposal site. City of Houston public guidance says commercial grease traps must be cleaned every 90 days unless Houston Health Department approves a waiver. This page is that checklist, plus phone numbers copied from each company’s own site.

Houston operator list · Questions owners actually ask

Ask on the quote

  1. Full evacuation of the trap, or a skim?
  2. Waste manifest with date, gallons, hauler, and disposal site — or an invoice only?
  3. Who keeps the copy, and for how long do they say you should?
  4. Inside Houston city limits, or a suburb with a different interval?
  5. Pump-out only, or an interceptor repair/replace with a plumber and a permit?
